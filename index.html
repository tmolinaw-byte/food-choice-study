Food Choice Study

Welcome & Informed Consent

You are invited to participate in a research study about food and beverage preferences among college students. This study is conducted by Aniket Sengupta Ph.D at University of the Andes Bogota.

What to expect:

  • The study takes approximately 10–12 minutes to complete.
  • You will answer brief questions about yourself and your lifestyle.
  • You will view snack and drink options and make a selection.
  • All responses are anonymous; no personally identifying information is collected.
  • Once the survey is started, it must be completed. If you do not submit, your responses will not count toward the research.
  • Data will be used solely for academic research purposes.
  • Questions? Contact: a.sengupta@uniandes.edu.co
